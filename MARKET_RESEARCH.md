## Signals and user needs
- Capterra inspection category listing (updated 25 Nov 2026) identifies mobile access, failed inspection tracking, photos, reports, real-time data and CAPA as prominent category features. It includes ratings/review counts but those are platform-specific and should not be treated as a representative market survey.
- Capterra small-business category lists products with features such as image capture, mobile access, failed inspections, report templates and work-order capabilities; pricing varies from low-cost per user to quote-based offerings.
- Offline reliability is a meaningful evaluation point: recent comparative material warns to test actual capture, photos and synchronization after reconnecting, rather than rely on a generic “offline” claim.
- Official GoAudits pricing page says its plans include online/offline workflows, instant reports and action plans, with annual per-user price points and a trial; its feature matrix includes role/permissions, scheduling, notifications, dashboards, export/API.

## Limits
This scan does not include live demos of every competitor, paid account trials, review-text coding, verified customer interviews, market-size calculations, or trademark search. Some sources are vendors or affiliate/comparison sites. Use this as discovery, not a validated TAM/SAM/SOM estimate. Claims and prices must be checked again before launch.

## Opportunity hypothesis
An underserved buyer may be small/mid-size multi-site cleaning, facilities and service contractors who need audit evidence and action closure but don't need enterprise GRC. The moat will not be checklist features alone; it should be low-friction rollout, customer-specific templates, integrations, reporting, trust/security and support.
