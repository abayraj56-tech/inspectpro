# Product definition

## Intended users
Frontline inspectors, site supervisors, facilities/operations managers, compliance leads and multi-site business owners.

## Pain points to validate
Paper records fragmented between teams; photos detached from findings; missed follow-ups; report assembly delays; checklist consistency across locations; cost/seat complexity; onboarding friction for occasional users; offline coverage; difficulty seeing overdue corrective actions.

## Differentiation hypothesis
A fast QR-to-browser flow, easy occasional-user participation, fixed transparent team/site pricing, action lifecycle and industry-specific ready-to-use templates. These are hypotheses; validate with 10–15 customer interviews and pilots before investing further.

## MVP implemented
Login; dashboard; templates list/create; checklist inspection submission; Pass/Fail/N/A; notes; automatic actions on failed checks; actions list and status update; dashboard metrics; CSV export; audit log endpoint; demo site and template; local text queue fallback.

## Roadmap
P0: tenant isolation, secure invite/password reset, photo evidence upload, offline sync reliability, PDF report, action verification policy, notifications, template scheduling, user/site admin.
P1: QR code per site/asset/template, signatures, geolocation opt-in, multilingual templates, analytics filters, bulk import/export, webhook/API keys.
P2: AI checklist creation, voice-to-report, image triage with human confirmation, integrations, template marketplace, white-label.

## Pricing test hypothesis (GBP, monthly)
- Solo/Starter £19: 1 site, 3 managers, limited monthly inspections, template starter pack.
- Team £59: 5 sites, 10 users, actions, exports, standard templates.
- Growth £149: 25 sites, 30 users, multi-site dashboard, scheduling, integrations.
- Enterprise: quote for SSO, dedicated tenancy, custom retention, support and SLA.
Price per site or team may be easier to sell to occasional inspectors than strict per-seat billing. Test both. These are proposed prices, not researched vendor price claims.

## Target launch niche
Cleaning and facilities contractors with 3–30 sites; then property maintenance and hospitality. A narrowly focused launch template library and customer case studies may outperform a generic positioning.
